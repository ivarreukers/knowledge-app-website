Learn it once. Keep it for good.

Most learning apps let you read and forget. Munin is a microlearning app built around spaced repetition, the memory technique used by medical students and language learners.

1

Short lessons

Every lesson takes a few minutes and ends with a short quiz. The same quick hit as a feed, except every topic is hand-curated and fact-checked, written for adults who want depth.

2

Smart review

Every question you answer joins your personal review queue. Get it right and it comes back later. Get it wrong and it comes back sooner. Sessions are sized to what is actually due, so catching up never feels like homework.

3

Your knowledge map

Every topic you study becomes a tile on a growing hex map. Streaks, XP and ranks turn showing up daily into a habit, and mastered topics go on your trophy shelf.

Type it, don't tap it.

Recognising the right option in a list is easy. Recalling it is what makes it stick. Munin asks you to type selected answers, and questions you already know well switch to typed recall in review. Small typos are forgiven and the official spelling is shown.

Why “Munin”?

In Norse mythology, Odin keeps two ravens: Huginn, thought, and Muninn, memory. Every morning Muninn flies out over the world and returns with what he has learned.

That is what this app does for you: it sends your curiosity out during the day and makes sure what it brings back is still there tomorrow.
